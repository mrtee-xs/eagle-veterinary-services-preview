Frequently Asked Questions

Practical advice from our veterinary team for dog and cat parents in Lagos.

Which vaccines does my puppy need in Lagos? ▼
Puppies should begin their core DHPPiL shots (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza, Leptospirosis) at 6–8 weeks with booster shots every 3–4 weeks until 16 weeks, followed by an annual Rabies vaccination. We issue and stamp official veterinary health passports at every stage.
What are the requirements for boarding my pet at Eagle Vet? ▼
All boarding pets must have an up-to-date vaccination record (DHPPiL and Rabies) and must be free of active tick or flea infestations. If treatments are due, our clinic can administer them upon check-in to guarantee a safe, hygienic environment for all boarders.
Can I walk in without an appointment for emergencies? ▼
Yes. Walk-in urgent care cases are prioritized immediately during clinic hours (Monday – Saturday: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM). If you are en route with an injured or poisoned pet, send a quick WhatsApp to +234 703 361 3617 so our clinical team prepares the surgical suite in advance.
How often should adult dogs be dewormed? ▼
In Lagos, adult dogs should routinely be dewormed every 3 months due to local environmental exposure, soil parasites, and humidity. Outdoor guard dogs or working dogs may require more frequent protocols as advised by our attending veterinarian.
